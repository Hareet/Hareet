## 👋 About Me

Hi there! I'm **Hareet Sandhu**, an Applied AI Engineering Lead based in Denver, Colorado.

I spent years as the SRE lead behind national-scale deployments of the [Community Health Toolkit](https://communityhealthtoolkit.org), the open-source platform six governments use to run their national community health systems. Today I lead applied AI at Medic: working out *when* AI is worth deploying, then making it cost-effective, model-agnostic and locally owned for the health programs and workers who rely on it.

> In a world where we can build much faster, we need to think harder about what we choose to build and to ensure that we build it safely, securely and focused on the direct user impact.

**🔭 What I Do**

- **Current Focus**: Buildtime AI for developers, Runtime AI on offline Android phones, and agentic infrastructure ops
- **Buildtime AI**: designed, architected and built [cht-agent](https://github.com/medic/cht-agent), a multi-agent system in production for cht-core and cht-conf, with human validation checkpoints
- **Runtime AI**: designed Voice for CHT (speak a visit and the form fills in offline, on models fine-tuned to ~273 MB for 4 GB Android phones), and designed and architected Smart Task Prioritization, the project behind Medic's AWS IMAGINE Grant 2025 win
- **No-Code CHT Configuration**: designed and architected tooling that lets non-technical health program staff configure CHT (AWS Breaking Barriers 2026 finalist)
- **Agentic SRE**: built an on-call agent squad (`agent-watchdog`, `claude-medic-aws`) with read-only access, sandboxed environments and a human in the loop
- **Past Experience**: SRE lead for national-scale CHT rollouts such as Kenya's eCHIS, leading the infrastructure and application architecture decisions as CHT moved from 3.x to 4.x; trained and localized DevOps practices with six governments' health ministries for full ownership and handoff
- **Strategy**: co-authored Medic's AI strategy with our CEO and lead its implementation

**🚀 Also Building**

- [hareet.ai](https://hareet.ai): an AI twin of me, built in the open: local models first, a capped cloud fallback, a real-time cost meter and a daily kill switch
- [Denver Local AI Solutions](https://denverlocal.ai): a company my wife and I run in Denver, setting up private AI in small teams' offices for their sensitive data

**💡 What I Like**

- 🧭 Deciding *when* AI is worth it: deterministic approaches first, and models small enough to run where the users are
- 📈 Treating observability and cost as first-class signals: Langfuse traces, Grafana dashboards and real-time cost meters
- 🔐 Least privilege for humans and agents alike: read-only by default, sandboxed, a human in the loop
- 🤖 Spec-driven, AI-native development: agents in named roles do most of the typing, and only I merge
- 🏂 Snowboarding the trees on Winter Park's back side, reached by bike and train from Denver
- 🚲 Exploring Denver's neighborhoods by bike year-round, plus a one-wheel
- 🐕 Hiking and camping with Arya, my 13-year-old Duck Toller
- 🌍 Work travel across Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia, most recently Senegal and Nigeria
